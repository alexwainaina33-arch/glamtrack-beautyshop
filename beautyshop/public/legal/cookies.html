Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar local storage technologies SalesTrack uses, and why. We've kept this short because, honestly, we don't use very many.

The short version: we don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your browsing data to anyone. The few things we store locally on your device exist to keep you logged in and let the app work offline.

1. What we use, and why

WhatPurposeType
Authentication sessionKeeps you logged in between visits, so you don't have to sign in every timeEssential
Offline sales queueStores sales made while you're offline (in-browser database) so they can sync once you're back onlineEssential — functional
Cached products & categoriesLets the point-of-sale screen keep working without an internet connectionEssential — functional
PWA install statusRemembers whether you've installed SalesTrack to your home screen, so we don't keep askingFunctional
Dismissed banners / tipsRemembers if you've closed a banner or seen a tip, for that sessionFunctional

2. What we do NOT use

  • No third-party advertising cookies
  • No cross-site tracking pixels
  • No sale of your browsing behavior to data brokers or ad networks
  • No social media tracking cookies embedded on your dashboard (our share buttons link out directly to WhatsApp, Facebook, etc. — they don't track you while you're using SalesTrack)

3. Third-party services

Some parts of the Service load resources from third parties, which may set their own minimal technical cookies:

  • Google Fonts — used to display our typography
  • M-Pesa (Safaricom Daraja) — used only during payment, on Safaricom's own payment pages

These providers are responsible for their own cookie practices on their own pages.

4. Managing local storage

Because we use local storage for essential functions like offline sales and staying logged in, clearing your browser's site data for SalesTrack will log you out and may affect any sales that haven't yet synced. We recommend only clearing this if instructed by our support team, or after confirming any pending offline sales have synced.

5. Changes to this policy

If what we store locally changes — for example, if we add a new feature that needs local storage — we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
