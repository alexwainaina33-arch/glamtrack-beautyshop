FAQ

Questions answered

Do I need internet to use SalesTrack? +
SalesTrack works best online, and a standard 3G mobile data connection is more than enough — there's no need for Wi-Fi or a fast connection. The POS also has an offline mode: sales made without internet are queued and sync automatically when connectivity returns.
Can I use SalesTrack on my phone? +
Yes — SalesTrack is designed specifically for smartphones. 95% of our customers run their entire business from one Android phone. It also works on tablets and desktop, but everything is optimised for one-handed mobile use first.
How does the free trial work? +
Register with your email and business details — no card required at any point during the trial. You get 7 full days to explore every feature. When the trial ends, choose a plan and pay via M-Pesa. If you don't subscribe, the app switches to read-only mode so your data is never lost.
Can I import my existing products? +
Yes. SalesTrack has a bulk CSV import built in. Prepare a spreadsheet with product names, prices, costs, barcodes and stock quantities — upload it and everything is imported in seconds. Our team can also help with the import free of charge.
Is my business data safe? +
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We take daily backups and maintain 99.9% uptime. Transaction records are immutable — no user (including you) can edit or delete a completed sale. This protects you from staff fraud and is a legal requirement for eTIMS compliance. You can export all your data at any time.
Can I manage multiple branches? +
Multi-branch management is available on the Enterprise plan. Each branch has its own inventory, staff, sales history and reports — all visible from one owner dashboard. You can switch between branches in one tap.
How do I get support? +
Email us at info@doublexsoftware.com — we respond within 24 hours. Growth and Enterprise customers get priority support with faster response times. We also offer free onboarding help for new shops including product import assistance.
