FAQ

Questions answered

Do I need internet to use SalesTrack? +
SalesTrack works best with an internet connection, which is needed to sync data and process M-Pesa payments. A stable mobile data connection (3G or better) is sufficient. Offline mode is on our roadmap.
Can I use SalesTrack on my phone or tablet? +
Yes. SalesTrack is fully responsive and works on any device — desktop, laptop, tablet or smartphone. Many businesses use it on a tablet at the counter and a phone for management on the go.
How does the 14-day free trial work? +
Register with your email, watch the quick tutorial, pick a plan and enter your card details — but nothing is charged for 14 days. Cancel anytime before that and you pay nothing at all.
Can I import my existing products? +
Yes. SalesTrack has a bulk CSV import feature built in. Prepare a spreadsheet with your product names, prices, costs, barcodes and stock quantities — upload it and everything is imported in seconds.
Is my data safe? +
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We take daily backups and maintain 99.9% uptime. Your data belongs to you — you can export everything at any time.
Can I manage multiple branches? +
Multi-branch management is available on the Enterprise plan. Each branch has its own inventory, staff, sales history and reports — all visible from one owner dashboard.
How do I get support? +
Email us at info@doublexsoftware.com and we respond within 24 hours. Growth and Enterprise customers get priority support with faster response times.
